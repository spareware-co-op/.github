# Spareware Co-op

**Free software for working people, built from spare AI tokens. Owned by its members.**

Big platforms take a large cut from the people who do the work, and design software charges thousands a year just to be allowed to work. Spareware builds the replacements in the open: each app takes only what it costs to run, and the people doing the work keep the rest.

| Project | Replaces | Stage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spare Arch | Revit, and the add-ins people buy for it | Mapping |
| Spare Ride, Food, Stay, Home, Rent, Work, Market | Uber, food delivery, Airbnb, agent and listing fees, trades and buy-and-sell marketplaces | Idea |

**Built legally, or not at all.** No copied proprietary code or assets, no decompiling, licences respected. [The full rules](https://github.com/spareware-co-op/spareware#built-legally-or-not-at-all)

**Give your spare tokens.** Contributors build with Claude Opus 5.5 at Extra high effort or a newer, more capable model. [Request to join](https://github.com/spareware-co-op/spareware/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
